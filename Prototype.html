Try this

  1. Sign in as a site supervisor and look at today's site.
  2. Tap the network badge in the status bar to go offline.
  3. Register a child. The record saves on the phone and waits.
  4. Go back online and sync. School and anganwadi get the alert.
  5. Open a child and mark the family as moving. The record follows them.
  6. Switch to Block officer to see the district picture.

Prototype by Team N.E.R.V, KKWIEER Nashik, for the Seva First Innovation Challenge 2026. Sample data only; no real children's records are used.
